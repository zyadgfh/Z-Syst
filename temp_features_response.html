PharmaSync Features

A complete pharmacy management system built to operate offline, manage multiple branches, and handle every core pharmacy workflow from sales to inventory, finance, CRM, and reporting.

Offline & Remote Access

  • Offline Desktop Application for full pharmacy operations without internet.
  • Mobile App for Remote Monitoring to track sales, inventory, and performance.
  • Remote Desktop for Supervisors to manage branches and purchase requirements.
  • Multi-Device Network System with local sync across devices.

Inventory & Product Intelligence

  • Real-time inventory tracking with expiration alerts and automated reordering.
  • Up-to-Date Drug & Price Database pre-loaded with Egyptian drug pricing and barcodes.
  • Integrated Drug Directory searchable by name, barcode, or scientific name.
  • Price Update Notifications with review before system changes apply.

Sales, Orders & Delivery

  • Fast POS system with multiple order statuses and delivery tracking.
  • General Sales Returns workflow for walk-ins, damaged goods, and refunds.
  • Delivery Management with delivery person tracking and pending order alerts.
  • Medication Dose Printing from open orders with client and doctor details.

Customer & Partner Management

  • Client Management CRM with credit control and purchase history.
  • Loyalty Points System to reward repeat customers.
  • Client Offers & Group Discounts that apply automatically.
  • Contracts & Insurance Companies management with claims tracking.

Branch & Group Pharmacy Control

  • Multi-Branch Monitoring from a single dashboard.
  • Group Pharmacy Item Search and internal stock transfers.
  • Group Pharmacy Supply Requests and order transfers.
  • Branch Communication for real-time collaboration.

Finance & Accounting

  • Complete financial management with transaction monitoring and summaries.
  • Expense and revenue tracking outside of normal operations.
  • Salary & Payroll Management with payment history.
  • Audit Logs & Balance Tracking for accountability and compliance.

Smart Automation & Alerts

  • AI-Powered Assistant with smart search, voice commands, and predictive insights.
  • AI-Powered Supply Orders for data-driven purchasing suggestions.
  • Repeated Orders & Smart Reminders for prior notice and missing items.
  • Smart Alerts for low stock, expiry, and critical business events.

Why PharmaSync?

PharmaSync is built for pharmacies that need powerful offline operation, multi-branch coordination, and a modern experience across desktop and mobile. It reduces manual work, keeps inventory accurate, and creates visibility for supervisors and branch managers.
