# PharmaSync Product Feature List

## Overview
PharmaSync is a complete pharmacy management system designed for offline-first desktop operations, remote monitoring, and multi-branch coordination. It combines inventory, sales, purchasing, finance, CRM, and analytics in one modern platform built for Egyptian pharmacies.

## Offline & Remote Access
- Offline Desktop Application: run the entire pharmacy system without internet access. All sales, inventory, reports, and daily operations work fully offline.
- Mobile App for Remote Monitoring: monitor sales, inventory, and performance in real time from your phone.
- Remote Desktop for Supervisors: full desktop application access for remote management, purchase planning, and oversight.
- Multi-Device Network System: connect multiple local devices with real-time sync for branch-level operations.

## Core Pharmacy Operations
- Inventory Management: real-time stock tracking with expiration alerts and automated reordering.
- Sales & Orders Management: fast POS workflows, multiple order statuses, delivery tracking, and quick checkout.
- Purchase Management: supplier and purchase order management with cost tracking, receiving, and returns.
- Warehouse & Stores Management: manage multiple warehouses, track expired items, sold items, and store analytics.
- Stock Movement Tracking: full visibility into every product movement over time for better purchasing and stock balance.
- Stock Reconciliation & Counting: physical inventory counts with automatic surplus/deficit detection and reason tracking.
- Opening Balances: import existing stock with quantities, costs, and expiry dates for smooth migration.

## Pricing, Drugs, and Product Intelligence
- Up-to-Date Drug & Price Database: pre-loaded database with the latest medications, prices, and barcode data for Egypt.
- Integrated Drug Directory: search any drug by name, barcode, or scientific name with current pricing, alternatives, and details.
- Price Update Notifications: receive alerts when drug prices change and review updates before applying them.
- Targeted Drug Discounts & Offers: assign special discounts to specific drugs or customer groups automatically at checkout.
- Advanced Contract & Company Pricing: manage contract pricing per supplier and override standard prices accurately.

## Customer & Partner Management
- Client Management: CRM with credit control, purchase history, loyalty points, and client-specific offers.
- Client Offers & Group Discounts: run promotions with percentage or fixed discounts for individuals or groups.
- Loyalty Points System: earn and redeem points for repeat customers to encourage retention.
- Doctors Management: add doctors, link prescriptions and medications, and track prescription history.
- Partners Management: manage business partners, relationships, and agreements in one place.
- Contracts & Insurance Companies: manage insurance contracts, track claims, and maintain claim logs.

## Finance & Accounting
- Financial Management & Accounting: track wallets, transactions, financial summaries, and real-time financial status.
- Money Transactions Monitoring: monitor every incoming and outgoing transaction for full financial safety.
- Additional Expenses & Revenues: record all extra costs and income outside sales and purchases.
- Salary & Payroll Management: manage employee compensation, salary payments, and payment history.
- Audit Logs & Balance Tracking: preserve a complete audit trail for inventory movement, balances, and financial adjustments.
- Financial Notification Improvements: log every financial adjustment with who made it, when, and why.

## Smart Automation & Alerts
- AI-Powered Assistant: intelligent automation with smart search, voice commands, and predictive insights.
- AI-Powered Supply Orders: automatic purchase recommendations based on sales, stock levels, and item movement.
- Repeated Orders & Smart Reminders: schedule recurring orders and receive notifications before they are due.
- Smart Alerts & Notifications: automatic warnings for expirations, low stock, shortages, and business-critical events.
- Inventory Safety Alerts: get proactive warnings for near-expiry items, critical stock levels, and unsafe inventory.

## Group & Branch Management
- Branch Communication: real-time messaging and coordination between branches.
- Multi-Branch Monitoring: single dashboard visibility for sales, inventory, and performance across branches.
- Group Pharmacy Item Transfers: move stock between pharmacies in the group to reduce shortages.
- Group Pharmacy Item Search: search inventory across group pharmacies instantly.
- Group Pharmacy Supply Requests: create and manage internal supply requests across branches.
- Group Pharmacy Order Transfers: transfer customer orders between pharmacies when one branch cannot complete fulfillment.

## Sales, Delivery, and Returns
- Delivery Management: manage delivery personnel, track out-for-delivery orders, and monitor pending deliveries.
- General Sales Returns: process returns without the original invoice for walk-ins, damaged goods, and refunds.
- Medication Dose Printing: print personalized dosage slips with client and doctor information from open orders.
- Barcode Login: staff can sign in quickly using a barcode for faster user switching.

## Client Intelligence & Attention
- Top Buyer Clients: identify highest-value clients with period comparison badges.
- Clients Needing Attention: surface clients with declining activity before they are lost.
- Top Referral Doctors: identify doctors driving the most orders and revenue.
- Doctors Needing Attention: detect referral drop-offs and prioritize follow-ups.
- Client Profile: 360° client view with balance, loyalty, last visit, frequency, and notes.
- Smart Client Insight at Checkout: customer stats surface automatically during sales.
- Client Attention Alerts: automated management alerts for clients with declining activity.
- Doctor Attention Alerts: notifications when referral doctor activity drops.

## Integrations & Usability
- Modern Design & Ease of Use: browser-like interface for effortless navigation and parallel operations.
- Smart Integrations: connect PharmaSync with existing tools and systems for automatic data flow.
- Data Import & Export: import products and store items from files; export reports, invoices, and analytics in PDF and Excel.
